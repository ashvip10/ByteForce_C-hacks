# ByteForce – 2D Cleanliness Adventure Game

## 🎮 About the Project
ByteForce presents an interactive 2D adventure game designed to promote cleanliness and environmental awareness through fun, engaging gameplay.

Players take control of a character who explores the environment, completes cleanliness missions, and learns the importance of keeping their surroundings clean.

## 🚀 Play the Game
**[Click here to play the game](https://ai.studio/apps/de20af72-8320-4309-b315-d1f613cd793b)**

## 🎯 Game Objective
The main objective is to encourage players to:
- Keep their surroundings clean.
- Identify and collect waste.
- Dispose of garbage responsibly.
- Complete cleanliness-related missions.
- Develop good environmental habits.

## ✨ Key Features
- **2D Gameplay:** Interactive 2D game environment.
- **Playable Character:** Control a character throughout the game.
- **Cleanliness Missions:** Complete tasks related to cleanliness and waste management.
- **Interactive Experience:** Explore the game and accomplish objectives.
- **Environmental Awareness:** Learn the importance of hygiene and a clean environment.

## 🕹️ How to Play
1. Open the game using the link above.
2. Start the game.
3. Follow the on-screen instructions.
4. Complete the cleanliness missions.
5. Explore, interact, and achieve your objectives.

## 🌍 Our Mission
Our mission is to make cleanliness awareness engaging and enjoyable. Through gaming, we aim to encourage everyone to take responsibility for maintaining a clean and healthy environment.
